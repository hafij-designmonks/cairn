Take a closer look at what holds the Cairn 01 together.

  1. Frame. A boxed steel ladder frame, welded by hand on the ridge and warranted for forty years.

    40 yrsframe warranty

  2. Suspension. Coil springs at all four corners and remote-reservoir shocks give the wheels fourteen inches of travel.

    14 inof wheel travel

  3. Drivetrain. A two-speed transfer case with a 4:1 low range, and lockers front and rear for the loose stuff.

    4:1low range

  4. Roll cage. Tubular steel over the cabin, so the roof and doors come off in minutes and you stay covered.

Meet the owners.

The first car I fixed.

Maya bought her Cairn with no idea how a transfer case worked. Two winters later she carries her own spares to trailheads across Colorado, swaps a shock in a car park and drives home. The open manual lives in the glovebox, folded at the axle pages.

Cairn 01 · Rust

Six weeks, no tarmac.

Tomas and Ines followed the Baja coast for six weeks on sand, washboard and dry riverbed, and never once touched a paved road. Most nights ended the same way: the roof tent up on a bluff above the sea, a small fire, and the last light going down over the water.

Cairn 01 · Sand

Home by the bothy light.

Rafe spends most weekends on the old drove roads of the Scottish Highlands. His Cairn fords the burns after rain, climbs into the mist and is parked by the bothy at dusk, one lamp lit in the window.
